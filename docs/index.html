About this database

The Mega Projects Open Database is a community-maintained, transparent record of how large infrastructure, energy, and industrial projects perform on cost and schedule — what they were planned to cost and finish, versus what they actually did.

The goal is to make the cost-and-schedule history of mega projects easier to look up, compare, and reason about — for researchers, journalists, policymakers, and anyone curious.

Scope

  • Sectors: Infrastructure (transport, water, civic), Energy & power, Industrial & manufacturing
  • Geography: US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, New Zealand (initial focus)
  • Threshold: ~$1B (USD or CAD) total project cost
  • Time: projects from any era, with active research focused on recent completions

Methodology

Each entry is sourced from at least one public document — government regulator filings, audit reports, project owner press releases, or major media reporting. Where possible, dates and costs are cross-checked against multiple sources.

How to contribute

Got a correction or a missing project? Open a Pull Request on the GitHub repo with the change and a primary source link in the commit message.
